Congratulations to David Surovik and Kun Wang, for the paper

“Any-axis Tensegrity Rolling via Bootstrapped Learning and Symmetry Reduction”
by David Surovik, Jonathan Bruce, Kun Wang, Massimo Vespignani and Kostas Bekris,

which has been accepted to appear at the upcoming International Symposium on Experimental Robotics (ISER) 2018, taking place this coming November in Buenos Aires, Argentina.

This is a collaborative effort with NASA Ames researchers Jonathan Bruce and Massimo Vespignani, who are leading tensegrity work at the research center in California.

ISER 2018 website: http://www.iser2018.org/
