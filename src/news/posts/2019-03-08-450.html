Edgar Granados, an MS student from the Departament of Digital Systems of the Instituto Tecnológico Autónomo de México (ITAM), is joining the PRACSYS lab over the summer months of 2018. Edgar is a member of Prof. Marco MoralesRobotics Lab at ITAM. Edgar will be working on motion planning methods for autonomous vehicle navigation.
