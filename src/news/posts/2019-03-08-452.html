The IJCAI/ECAI conference is taking place in Stockholm, Sweden between July 13-19, 2018. Kostas Bekris participated in the conference and presented during the Robotics and Planning session on Wednesday, July 18 the following paper:

Fast Model Identification via Physics Engines for Data-Efficient Policy Search 
by Shaojun Zhu, Andrew Kimmel, Kostas E. Bekris, Abdeslam Boularias

This effort relates to the project of closing the reality gap between physics-based simulation and reality.
