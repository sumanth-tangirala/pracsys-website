I’m a postdoc working with Kostas Bekris and Jingjin Yu at Rutgers University.

I’m interested in algorithmic and computational challenges in planning for robotics, mobility, and related multi-agent settings. My PhD research focuses on coordinated motion planning for multiple moving agents/bodies. I’ve been deepening our understanding of what makes core problems computationally hard, thereby identifying new algorithms and tractability frontiers towards more effective practical solutions.

I recently completed my PhD at the Computational Geometry Lab at Tel Aviv University, which is led by Dan Halperin, where I also completed my MSc. My BSc is in Computer Science, at Tel Aviv University as well.
